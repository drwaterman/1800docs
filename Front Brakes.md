The 1800 series came with 4 wheel disc brakes starting in 1970 and are fairly well regarded, being well balanced and capable of locking the wheels, but I wanted to upgrade what could easily and affordably be changed over when replacing the work stock components. I found [an old forum thread](https://www.swedespeed.com/threads/72-p1800-es-searching-for-front-brake-rotors-discs.195725/post-2159070) on SwedeSpeed that discussed upgrading to vented 240 rotors. I messaged the original poster and he was kind enough to send me the [CAD file for the adapter plate](images/Front%20Brakes/caliper%20adapter.dxf) he used. I had it laser cut out of 1/2" mild steel plate by SendCutSend. The price was reasonable, $68 plus tax for the pair, and that included tapping the threaded holes and finishing them in black oxide. I was happy with the experience and have ordered from them again since. 

One of the nice things about switching to 240 parts is they are much easier to find. The total production number for 1800s is under 50,000, while the 240 series is more like 2.8 million. The front calipers were the same for all of the years as long as the car wasn't equipped with ABS. My shopping list for the conversion was as follows:

* 240 vented non-ABS front calipers: Raybestos FRC3439 and FRC3440 remanufactured from RockAuto, $50 each including core charge
* 240 front rotors: R1 Concepts 631-27005L and 631-27005R from RockAuto, slotted and drilled, $57 each
* 240 brake pads: Actually uses the same pads as the 1800. I went with Porterfield GD549 pads in R4-S street performance compound, \$100 for 4

Considering that until recently stock front rotors for the 1800 were going for \$300 a set, and then for some time unavailable, I think a complete conversion to vented, slotted, drilled brakes for under \$400 is a good deal.

There were a few minor modifications required besides the adapter bracket. One was to open up the mounting holes on the calipers a hair by reaming with a 1/2" drill bit. I'm assuming the 240 uses metric mounting bolts. Another is to get 1/4" longer mounting bolts for the knuckle-caliper bracket assembly. Stock bolts are Volvo part numbers 955539 and 955535 which are 3/18-16x2-1/2" and 3/8-16x1-1/4". They should be changed to 2-3/4" and 1-1/2" long bolts.
